**Glorbo is a desktop pet that can:**

*Fly around and bounce off the screen
*Be thrown

*Abduct Cows (You can spawn a cow by right clicking Glorbo and cows also have a 1 / 33 chance of spawning every second)

*Be shaken and thrown out of its UFO (you have to shake it really hard for Glorbo to fall :P)

*If Glorbo falls out of its UFO it walks around till it finds its UFO and gets back inside (You can also throw Glorbo around at this state)


**How To Download:**

Click download on the link (https://blegghh.itch.io/glorbo) and open
